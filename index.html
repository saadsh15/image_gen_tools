About

A safe space for
your raw thoughts

Mood Dump isn't about perfection. It's about permission — permission to feel, to vent, to wonder, to rage, to hope. Pour out whatever's swirling in your head without worrying about grammar, judgment, or who might read it. This is your space. No filters, no audience, and zero trackers. Secured with end-to-end encryption, it's just you and the page.

How It Works

Three steps to clarity

1

Open the app

Launch Mood Dump whenever you feel the urge to write. No sign-up walls, just open and go.

2

Dump your thoughts

Let it all out. Type, scribble, rant — whatever your mind needs to release in this moment. We do not read your data.

3

Watch your story

Look back and see your patterns, growth, and journey unfold beautifully over time. Your data is encrypted.
